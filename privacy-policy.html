Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Welcome to AI Comment Moderator. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information when you use our Facebook Page connection and comment moderation features.

1. Information We Access

When you connect your Facebook account, the application may receive information necessary to manage the Facebook Pages that you are authorized to manage.

This may include:

  • Facebook account authorization information
  • Facebook Page name and Page ID
  • Page access information required for authorized actions
  • Comments and related information required for moderation

2. How We Use Information

Information accessed through Facebook is used only to provide the features of AI Comment Moderator, including:

  • Connecting a Facebook Page
  • Analyzing comments using our AI moderation model
  • Identifying potentially toxic, spam, promotional, or normal comments
  • Performing authorized moderation actions when enabled

3. Facebook Data

AI Comment Moderator uses Meta's platform and APIs to provide Facebook Page integration. We only request permissions that are necessary for the application's intended functionality.

We do not sell Facebook user data or use Facebook data for advertising purposes.

4. Access Tokens

Facebook access tokens are sensitive credentials. They are used only to perform authorized Facebook API operations required by the application.

Users should never share their Facebook passwords or access tokens with other people.

5. Data Storage

AI Comment Moderator is designed to minimize the storage of personal information. Information may be temporarily processed when required to provide moderation functionality.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties. Information may only be processed through services required to operate the application and its authorized integrations.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect application credentials and information. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. User Control

Users can stop using the application and remove its Facebook permissions through their Facebook account settings.

9. Children's Privacy

AI Comment Moderator is not intended for children under the age required by applicable laws to use the service.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application or its features change. Updates will be published on this page.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the application, please contact the application developer.

12. Third-Party Services

The application may use Meta/Facebook services and APIs. Their use is also subject to Meta's applicable terms and policies.
